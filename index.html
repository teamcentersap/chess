Privacy Policy

Chess King Escape is an offline chess and puzzle game. This notice explains what is stored on the device and what is not collected.

App Store privacy labels

For this release you can declare Data Not Collected in App Store Connect if nothing leaves the device. We do not collect name, email, location, contacts, or browsing history.

No account

You do not create an account or sign in. There is no player ID linked to a cloud profile.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising SDK, no IDFA / ATT tracking prompt, no cross-app tracking.

No in-app purchases

This release is free. No StoreKit purchases, no payment data.

Gameplay progress — stored only on this device

Level progress is saved locally so you can continue later. That typically includes:

  • Which Adventure levels you completed and star counts
  • Max unlocked level / continue point
  • Chase best wave / escapes / run counts
  • Daily challenge date and streak (device-local)
  • Classic Chess unfinished game (FEN / move list) if you resume
  • Settings: music, SFX, mute, haptics, high contrast, reduced motion, show threats, computer speed
  • Optional achievement unlock flags and simple play stats (moves, wins) on device

This progress is not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app usually deletes this local save.

What “levels” mean for privacy

Progress is optional product design. Completing every level is not required. Privacy only explains that any progress you choose to make stays on your device. Responsible play belongs in the Terms of Use.

Analytics / crash reports

This release does not include third-party analytics or crash SDKs that send gameplay or device identifiers to us.

Internet

Play does not require the internet. Opening the App Store page or OS updates is handled by Apple, not by our game servers.

Permissions

We do not request camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location to play.

Children

General audience chess / puzzles. No chat, no public UGC, no social graph.

Your choices

Clear progress by removing the app (or clearing app data via device settings where available). You can stop playing anytime.

Changes

If a future update adds online features, IAP, or analytics, we will update this policy and App Store privacy labels.

Contact

By using Chess King Escape you agree to play for personal, non-commercial entertainment and education. The app is provided “as is.”

Responsible play — not designed to be addictive

Chess King Escape is a calm puzzle / board game. It is not designed to push endless spending, gambling, or compulsive play.

There are no loot boxes, no real-money gambling, no random paid rewards, and no pressure to keep buying content.

Completing all Adventure levels is optional. Nothing in the app requires you to clear every world, chase wave, or daily streak.

Please take breaks. If you feel tired or frustrated, pause, switch modes, or put the device down. Play should stay enjoyable.

Healthy habits

Set your own limits — for example, a few puzzles, one Classic game, or a short Chase run.

Use device Screen Time / Downtime tools if you want system-level limits.

This app does not use dark patterns that hide the exit, force sessions, or punish you for leaving.

Fairness

Adventure levels are designed to be solvable. Classic Chess follows standard rules. Outcomes are based on play, not hidden pay-to-win.
